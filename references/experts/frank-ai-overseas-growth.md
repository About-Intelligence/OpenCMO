## Core thesis: AI product growth must center on paid conversion

AI products have a fatal problem traditional SaaS doesn't: **the more users, the higher the cost** (inference cost scales linearly with DAU).

> "If your growth strategy only pours users in without caring whether they're willing to pay, you're not growing — you're accelerating your losses."

Every registered-but-non-paying user = continuous token burn + zero revenue. So the growth goal must be **finding users willing to pay**, not maximizing signups.

## The Four-Pillar Growth System

The four channels form a **closed loop that feeds data into one another**, not four independent pipelines.

### Pillar 1: SEM — a validation tool, not an acquisition tool

- Purpose: quickly validate which keywords attract users who will pay
- **The optimization target must be paid conversion, not signups**
- Data produced by optimizing for signups will skew every downstream decision
- Output: identify the "gold-mine keywords" (high paid-conversion keywords)

### Pillar 2: SEO — building assets, not writing blogs

- Keyword selection basis: the high paid-conversion keywords already validated by SEM
- Logic: ads prove "people searching this keyword are willing to pay" → worth spending 3-6 months winning organic rankings
- Anti-pattern: picking keywords by copying competitors or by search volume = the direction may be wrong from day one

### Pillar 3: GEO — a free byproduct of SEO

- Context: ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews are eating traditional search traffic
- The content foundation comes from SEO (structured content, user case studies, expert reviews)
- **Do SEO well → GEO is nearly free; skip SEO → GEO has nothing to stand on**
- Monitor: how many times has your product appeared in ChatGPT/Perplexity answers?

### Pillar 4: Social — the ultimate KPI is branded-search volume

- Trap: chasing follower counts (correlation between followers and paying users is extremely low)
- Correct KPI: after publishing content, did branded-search volume on Google go up?
- Paid conversion rate of branded-search users >> ad-keyword users
- Essence: social media is a "brand search engine," not "content marketing"

## The closed data loop

```
SEM validates gold-mine keywords
    ↓
SEO builds deep content around those keywords
    ↓
GEO: AI search cites the content (free byproduct)
    ↓
Social distributes the same selling points → branded-search volume rises
    ↓
Branded search lifts SEO rankings + ad Quality Score
    ↓ (loop)
```

> "One keyword insight travels a full circle through four channels, and every step amplifies the effect of the one before it."

---

## Why most teams can't build this system

- **Very few people understand all four channels**: ad buyers don't know SEO, SEO people don't look at ad data, social teams don't know branded-search volume
- **Outsourcing can't fix it**: each agency only manages its own KPI; the data never connects
- You need **one person who can string the four threads together**, not four vendors

## Self-audit checklist

- [ ] Is Google Ads optimizing for paid conversion (not signups)?
- [ ] Is SEO keyword selection based on SEM validation (not competitors or search volume)?
- [ ] Do you know how often your product appears in ChatGPT/Perplexity?
- [ ] Is social tracking branded-search volume (not followers and engagement)?
- [ ] When did your ads/SEO/social teams last share data?
