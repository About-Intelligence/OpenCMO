## Core thesis: Influencer marketing is the most underrated growth channel for early-stage PLG products

In its early days, Notion drove millions of signups through YouTube/TikTok creator partnerships. The key was not "paying big influencers to run ads," but building a system of **authentic usage → evergreen content → flywheel**.

---

## The path from zero to scale

### Phase 1: Discovery & small-budget validation
- Stumbled on YouTubers organically mentioning Notion in their videos
- Reached out proactively, offering small budgets for full dedicated reviews on "how to manage video production with Notion"
- Gave them UTM links to track signups
- Results were "incredible" → immediately asked to double the budget → same results → kept scaling

### Phase 2: Establishing operating rules
Three iron rules distilled from the early experiments:

| Rule | What | Why |
|------|------|-------|
| Full videos > 60-second ad spots | Full videos cost more, but CPA is significantly lower | A completely different level of user trust |
| Creators must genuinely use the product | Not have them "act" | Videos feel more authentic, barely reading as sponsored content |
| Minimum threshold of 50K followers | Focus resources on higher-impact creators | Only later expanded to micro-KOLs (especially on TikTok) |

### Phase 3: Scaling up
- Hired an external contractor to manage the whole process (sourcing, outreach, coordination)
- Freed internal bandwidth to focus on the Ambassador program, the template gallery, and user conferences
- Eventually upgraded into a full-time in-house function, hiring a dedicated influencer marketing lead

---

## Four core benefits (why this channel is special)

### 1. Evergreen Signups
- YouTube videos keep driving signups months after publishing
- CPA **keeps falling** over time (new signups ÷ fixed production cost)
- The longer it compounds, the better the ROI → well suited to early teams with limited resources

### 2. Break Into New Markets
- Partnered with international creators early on
- When multiple KOLs went live at the same time, overall signups in certain regions spiked **2-5x**
- The lowest-cost way to penetrate across languages and regions

### 3. Creator Snowball Effect
- Creators initially used Notion to manage video production → their first video shared that use case
- Other creators saw it → started using it too → more people proactively approached Notion to collaborate
- Notion's visibility within the creator community became self-reinforcing

### 4. Fuel Community Growth
- New users who arrived via KOLs actively sought out the community to participate
- Especially visible in new regions → community signups rose → more local Ambassadors

---

## The flywheel

```
KOL videos (authentic usage)
    ↓
New user signups (UTM-tracked) + evergreen long-tail traffic
    ↓
Community growth (Ambassadors, template contributors)
    ↓
More creators see Notion → reach out to collaborate
    ↓ (loop; CPA keeps falling)
```

---

## Practical advice for early teams

1. **Find a vertical niche** — don't spray and pray; focus on one segment first (Notion started in the productivity/education/tech circles)
2. **Chase authenticity** — get creators to actually use your product rather than just appearing on camera reading lines; when your product is little known early on, you need to get more creative about making creators excited
3. **Run experiments** — test formats, outreach approaches, and platforms; this can't be validated in a few days — it takes time
