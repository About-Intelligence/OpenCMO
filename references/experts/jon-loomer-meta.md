## Core thesis: understand the algorithm, don't fight it

> "Understand how the algorithm works and leverage it, rather than hoping for it to work a different way."

The core of Jon Loomer's methodology: most of an advertiser's intuitive moves (audience segmentation, placement control, complex structures) are negative optimizations against the modern Meta algorithm. **Only by understanding how the algorithm works can you know when to let go and when to intervene.**

---

## The five foundational principles (Common Sense Playbook)

### 1. The Algorithm is Literal

> "The algorithm will then do all it can to get as many of that action as it can within your budget."

The Performance Goal you set determines everything. Choose "traffic" and the system finds people who click; choose "conversions" and it finds people who pay. **Choosing the wrong goal is the root cause of most failed Meta campaigns** — not the creative, not the audience.

### 2. Targeting Obsession is Overrated

With Advantage+ Audience enabled, all an advertiser can truly control is: geography, exclusion audiences, and minimum age. Every other targeting input becomes a "suggestion" the algorithm is free to ignore.

> "Your targeting inputs just aren't that important."

This isn't giving up control: the algorithm is better at finding people than human judgment, provided you feed it enough conversion data.

### 3. Attribution is Your Responsibility

How well Meta performs for you depends directly on the quality of the data you feed it:
- Is the Pixel fully configured
- Is CAPI live (after the iOS restrictions, Pixel-only data severely underreports)
- Are conversion events accurately defined

The platform only mirrors reality: feed the algorithm bad data, and the results you get are trained on bad data.

### 4. Simplification Over Complexity

> "A complicated approach often works against you. It waters down your budget, drives up costs."

The carefully built "funnel structures," multiple ad sets split by audience, multiple campaigns split by placement — most of the time these compete against each other for the same audience while diluting each ad set's learning data.

### 5. Strategic Intervention

Neither "fully hands-off" nor "full control." Understanding how the algorithm works lets you judge:
- When to trust the algorithm (most of the time)
- When the algorithm has weaknesses that need human steering (specific scenarios)

## Eight mindset shifts ("I Was Wrong": the 2026 evolution of his methodology)

In his April 2026 article, Jon Loomer systematically reviewed how his own views changed; the same shifts happened across the industry:

| Topic | Old approach | Current approach | Why it changed |
|------|----------|----------|---------|
| **Remarketing** | Obsessed over fine-grained website custom audiences | Rely on Advantage+ algorithmic targeting | Audience quality dropped after iOS 14; the algorithm actually finds people better |
| **Targeting control** | Layered interests, behaviors, lookalikes | Minimize constraints, trust system expansion | The marginal value of manual targeting nearly vanished in the Advantage+ era |
| **Role of creative** | Believed good targeting could make mediocre creative work | Creative is the first lever, no exceptions | The Andromeda model routes different creatives to different audiences; creative diversity became key |
| **Campaign structure** | Built complex funnels segmented by audience | Radically simplified; the algorithm handles segmentation | Duplicate ad sets compete for the same audience and bid each other's prices up |
| **Placement control** | Turned off underperforming placements | Let Meta dynamically optimize placement allocation | Local data from a single placement doesn't reflect overall performance |
| **Automation** | Resisted CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization) | Embrace all automation, including budget allocation | The algorithm allocates budget better than humans do |
| **Advantage+ Creative** | Disliked it, worried it would alter the creative | Keep most enhancements on | Enhancements generate additional variants, not a uniform edit |
| **Learning phase** | Feared resetting the learning phase, afraid to touch live ads | No longer fixates on learning-phase status | Good results come from good creative, not "magic settings" |

## Core counterintuitive takes

**"You will be wrong"**
> "You will be wrong. If you are unable to embrace this possibility, you're likely to create a fantasy land where everyone else is to blame but you."

This is the bedrock attitude of Jon Loomer's entire methodology: the purpose of testing is to **falsify** your own hypotheses, not confirm them. The most common advertiser mistake is attributing failure to the platform or the audience instead of re-examining their own judgment.

**Meta's core direction of change in 2025** (based on the 83 changes):
> "Less advertiser control, more algorithm authority."

The shared direction across all 83 changes: Andromeda decides which ads enter the auction, Advantage+ decides targeting, GEM decides placement allocation, Value optimization decides bidding. The advertiser's control surface keeps narrowing, and the room left for manual operation keeps shrinking.
