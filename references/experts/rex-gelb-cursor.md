## Core theses (5)

1. **Ads amplify results — they don't create PMF**: running ads before retention and conversion are working only exposes problems faster, and creates a negative feedback loop — the algorithm learns the traits of churned users, and the more you spend, the worse it gets.

2. **90% of ads fail the moment they launch** (Rex shared this at HubSpot's INBOUND conference): the goal is to build a system that finds what works faster than your competitors do, not to "get it right the first time."

3. **In 2026, AI takes over bidding and targeting**: Google AI Max and Meta Advantage+ are already the defaults. The operator's role shifts from managing ad settings to guaranteeing the quality of the data fed to the system. **Only three levers remain under your control: creative quality, tracking data, and landing page structure**.

4. **Meta's algorithm favors authentic, native content**: content a founder shoots casually on a phone can outperform a two-week professional production. Short-form video CPV as low as $0.01–$0.02.

5. **The biggest LinkedIn mistake is adding complexity to the conversion path**: sending traffic to external landing pages significantly lowers conversion rates. Prioritize LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms (on-platform forms with auto-filled data).

## Timing (when to start)

**Signals you must have before entering**:
- Week-4 user retention > 20% (otherwise ads just amplify a "leaky bucket")
- Users willing to renew (otherwise continuous acquisition is itself a loss-making model)
- Organic word of mouth: real user referrals that don't depend on discounts

**Small-scale early testing is reasonable**:
- Use $2,000–$5,000 for user recruitment, demand validation, or audience targeting tests
- At this stage ads are for fast learning, not revenue

## Budget pacing

| Stage | Monthly budget | Goal |
|------|--------|------|
| Seed / pre-PMF validation | $0–$20,000 | Collect signals, validate feasibility |
| Series A | $20,000–$50,000, ramping → $100,000+ | Prove out the model, test 3–4 ad types |
| Series B and beyond | $200,000+ | Scale what works, expand channels and regions |

**Note**: paid results often take 2–3 months to stabilize; giving up too early means missing the opportunity.

## LinkedIn strategy

**Core principle**: convert on-platform first, minimize redirects.

**Recommended ad formats (by priority)**:
1. **Lead Gen Forms**: users submit directly in-platform with auto-filled data — highest conversion rate
2. **Thought Leadership Ads**: experience-sharing from founders/experts, CPC $5–$15, standout engagement rates
3. **Document Ads**: users can preview without leaving the platform; the lowest CPL on the platform
4. **In-feed Ads**: good for product demo requests, product launches, event promotion
5. **Message/Conversation Ads**: simulate a one-on-one sales conversation

**Multi-audience testing**: run the same ad against 3–4 different personas and let the data tell you who the real buyer is.

**2026 B2B benchmarks**:
- CPC: $5.50–$8.50
- CTR: 0.44%–0.65%
- ROAS: 4.1–8.3x

## Google Ads strategy

**The right order: Search first, then consider Display or YouTube**.

**Three core campaign types (in launch order)**:

1. **Brand**:
   - Purpose: protect the high-value traffic of users actively searching for you
   - Extremely cheap (low CPC, high CTR and conversion rates)
   - Run it even if you rank organically — otherwise competitors will push you down the page

2. **Competitor**:
   - One of the highest-ROI early test directions
   - Extremely high prospect quality (users are actively evaluating options)
   - Requirement: present a convincing differentiated advantage or a reason to migrate off the competitor

3. **Non-brand generic search**:
   - Category keywords — highly competitive and relatively expensive
   - Captures brand-new market demand

**Other formats (mid-to-late stage only)**: Performance Max, Display, YouTube — at the starting stage, search ads always come first.

## Meta strategy

**Core logic: intervene less, let the algorithm run**.

**In practice**:
- Combine Facebook + Instagram placements and let the system allocate automatically
- No need to manually split mobile/desktop
- Avoid overly small audiences (a 5,000-person target gives the model nothing to learn from)
- Recommended audience size: 50,000–500,000+

**2026 content trends**:
- The algorithm increasingly favors authentic, native, platform-style content
- Founder phone footage > professional production
- Short-form video CPV: $0.01–$0.02

**What to measure**: the ads' contribution to real sales opportunities, not just top-of-funnel impressions and clicks.

## Tracking setup (non-negotiable)

Incomplete tracking data (e.g. capturing only 40% of conversions) feeds the algorithm distorted data, and paid performance degrades systematically.

**2026 baseline setup**:
- **Server-side Tracking / CAPI**: bypass browser limitations, improve data completeness
- **Advanced Matching**: feed hashed user data back to ad platforms to improve attribution accuracy
- **Consent Mode**: keep capturing useful signal while staying privacy-compliant

## Counterintuitive takes (most worth remembering)

- **Don't run ads too early**: the most common mistake — most people start burning money before they have PMF
- **On LinkedIn, don't send traffic to external landing pages**: almost every team makes this mistake
- **Over-managing Meta actually makes it worse**: the finer you slice it, the worse it performs — loosen targeting and let the machine learn
- **Campaigns should be consolidated, not fragmented**: LinkedIn's Thought Leadership and Document Ads have far lower CPLs than regular in-feed ads
- **Don't only look at top-of-funnel metrics**: on Meta, measure contribution to the real sales pipeline
