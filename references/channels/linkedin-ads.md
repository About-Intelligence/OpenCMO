# LinkedIn Ads — Expert Knowledge Compendium

> ⚠️ **Freshness note**: platform mechanics, benchmarks, and pricing in this file were last verified in 2026. Ad platforms change quarterly — treat specific numbers as directional and re-verify against current platform documentation before acting.

## Expert Views

### Rex Gelb (Head of Paid Media, Cursor)
> LinkedIn now accounts for 39% of global B2B paid media budgets. Its advantage is the precision of professional data. The biggest mistake is artificially adding complexity to the conversion path.

**Core recommendations**:
- Prefer Lead Gen Forms (on-platform forms) over driving traffic to external landing pages
- Test the same ad against 3–4 different personas
- Prioritize testing Thought Leadership Ads and Document Ads

---

## Ad Format Comparison

| Format | Best use | Cost profile | Priority |
|------|---------|---------|--------|
| Lead Gen Forms | Any scenario requiring lead capture | Highest conversion rate | ⭐⭐⭐ First choice |
| Thought Leadership Ads | Personal brand / expert content | CPC $5–$15, high engagement rate | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| Document Ads | Content downloads, whitepapers | Lowest CPL on the platform | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| In-feed Ads | Product demos, launches, events | Standard | ⭐⭐ |
| Message/Conversation Ads | Simulate one-on-one sales | Pricier but high quality | ⭐⭐ |

---

## Targeting Strategy

**Test across dimensions**: job title, seniority, company size, industry, employees of specific companies

**Rex Gelb's advice**:
- The audience that ultimately converts best is often not the ICP you originally assumed
- Run the same ad against 3–4 different audiences and let the data speak

## Anti-Patterns (mistakes the experts have made)

- ❌ Sending LinkedIn traffic to external landing pages (every redirect leaks users)
- ❌ Drawing conclusions after testing only one audience
- ❌ Running only in-feed ads while ignoring Thought Leadership and Document Ads
- ❌ Not using Lead Gen Forms (adding unnecessary friction)
