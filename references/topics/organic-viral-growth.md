# Viral & Content Growth Strategy

## Aggregation Sites

### Zongyuan (YouMind): The Underlying Logic

> "The underlying traffic secret of aggregation sites: free, abundant, high-value resources. This is especially good at triggering spontaneous sharing by self-media accounts and KOLs. Users love it, so when a blogger posts it, it attracts engagement, boosts the post's reach, and grows their following."

**Five success factors**: fast, multilingual, continuously updated, high quality, distinctive UI

**Audience fit is the prerequisite**:
- Done right: YouMind's prompt aggregation site converts sign-ups at 20% (visitors are AI creators)
- Cautionary tale: Wordware got 8.1M UV with a 3–4% sign-up conversion rate (visitors were ordinary users)

### What is NOT an aggregation site

❌ A model-output comparison site: each entry is a comparison image, not "a resource users would want to bookmark," so KOLs won't share it

✅ The essence of an aggregation site: **a list of high-value resources users want to bookmark and share with others**

## Trend-Jacking Strategy

### Zongyuan's Methodology

**Core insight**: riding a trend = riding user demand; a trend is user demand erupting in concentrated form at a moment in time

**The Nano Banana 2 traffic-capture case**:
- Exact release time unknown → prepared 100 prompt-comparison pages in advance
- OpenClaw polled Vertex AI every 30 minutes → release triggered content production and distribution
- Result: all content auto-published within hours of the release

**The Seedance 2.0 pre-positioning case**:
- Built the landing pages before the model was even released
- Prepared the content skeleton based on assumptions
- After release, automatically collected discussion, generated articles, and auto-published

**Principle**: you don't need to ship on launch day, but **you must publish within the first week** (late sites get almost no traffic)

## Social Media Distribution

### Zongyuan's Experience

**Follower count vs distribution effect**:
| Followers | Per-post reach floor | Per-post reach ceiling |
|--------|------------|------------|
| Dozens | Single digits | — |
| Hundreds | Dozens | 50K |
| Thousands | Hundreds | 200K |

X (Twitter) impressions → product visitors (UV) conversion: 1–2% is already good

**The power of multilingual distribution**:
- Ship 16 languages → KOLs in different languages share spontaneously
- A Turkish KOC got 800K impressions on a single post, and posted twice
- 50+ time zones with over 1,000 visitors, 200+ time zones in total

## Viral Artifact: Turning the Product's Output Itself into Traffic (Phil Carter)

When the product itself feels enough "like magic," it carries its own distribution — users aren't promoting you; they're sharing something valuable to them.

**Canonical cases**:
- **Tolan**: generates a personality-match result image after onboarding → users spontaneously share it to social media → new users arrive
- **Gamma**: AI-generated decks / websites naturally flow out into meetings, search engines, and LLM answers — every use is a distribution event
- **Lovable** (Elena Verna): every user-generated web app carries a "built with Lovable" watermark → product-led virality

**How it differs from aggregation sites**: an aggregation site is a resource hub you deliberately build; a Viral Artifact arises naturally in the course of usage and is inherently worth sharing.

## AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

A new distribution channel: getting your product to appear inside answers from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and other AIs.

**The logic**: the way users get information is shifting from "Google search → click a link" to "ask an AI directly → get an answer." If the AI doesn't mention you when answering relevant questions, you're invisible on this channel.

**Relationship to GEO**: GEO (see [growth-101-four-pillars.md](../guides/growth-101-four-pillars.md), chapter 3) is an extension of SEO, specifically optimizing content to be cited by AI; AEO's emphasis is on the product brand itself appearing in AI answers.

**Reddit's new role**: Reddit has long been treated by large models as a major training-corpus source. Building genuine product discussion and user presence on Reddit shapes how LLMs mention your product — Reddit is turning from a "community platform" into an "entry point to AI training data."
