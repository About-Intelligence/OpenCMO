# Budget Allocation

## Monthly Budget Reference by Stage

### Rex Gelb's (Cursor) Experience

| Stage | Monthly budget range | Team setup | Focus |
|------|-----------|---------|------|
| Seed / pre-PMF | $0–$20,000 | 1 person on 1 product | Validate feasibility |
| Series A | $20,000–$50,000 → $100,000+ | Start watching the sales pipeline | Prove out 3–4 ad types |
| Series B and beyond | $200,000+ | Dedicated team | Scale what works; expand channels and geographies |

**Key milestone**: Rex himself started as one person, one product, $20,000/month — with the sole goal of validating whether paid worked at all.

## How to Split Across Channels

First-hand expert data is still missing here; the following is framework-level guidance (pending expert input):

### Rex Gelb's suggested testing order
1. Test LinkedIn + Google first (the highest-intent B2B channels)
2. Add Meta after validation (reach at scale)
3. Mid-to-late stage: consider YouTube, Reddit, Twitter/X, CTV (connected TV)

### The aggressive team's Series A path
- Early on: prove out 1–2 channels first
- Ramp monthly budget step by step to $100,000+
- This is often the key inflection point where the business takes off

## When to Scale a Working Channel

> "Once a channel is validated, expand aggressively as soon as possible — don't wait for the next cycle."
> — Rex Gelb

Ad costs keep rising (competition intensifies), so the first-mover advantage is significant.
